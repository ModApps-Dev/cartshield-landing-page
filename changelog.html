Version History

CartShield Changelog

Follow the latest feature releases, performance optimizations, and updates.

v2.4.0 — Latest Production Release October 2026

Engine performance upgrades, sub-2ms edge evaluation, and Shopify 2027-01 Checkout Extensibility compatibility.

  • Sub-2ms Wasm Optimization: Reduced WebAssembly execution footprint to under 2ms globally across Shopify edge infrastructure.
  • Express Checkout Parity: Enhanced synchronous enforcement across Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal Express checkouts.
  • Address Field Targeting: Direct red field highlight on Address Line 1 and Line 2 with localized customer guidance.
  • Military & Diplomatic Bases: Comprehensive pattern expansion for APO, FPO, and DPO military addresses.
v2.3.0 — B2B Wholesale & Carton Multiples August 2026

Introduced wholesale carton case multiples and dynamic remainder calculation for B2B merchants.

  • Master Carton Multiples: Enforce pack sizes (multiples of 6, 12, 24, etc.) to eliminate split-case warehouse repacking.
  • Dynamic Adjustment Notice: Tells buyers exactly how many units to add or subtract to meet minimum case requirements.
  • Collection-Specific Rules: Target rules by collections, vendor tags, or specific SKU subsets.
v2.2.0 — Sample Kit Guard & Rule Builder June 2026

Added sample kit loss-leader defense and interactive visual rule testing simulator.

  • Sample Kit & Add-on Guard: Prevent standalone purchases of loss-leader sample items without qualifying parent products.
  • Visual Rule Builder: Test checkout rules in real time inside the merchant admin before publishing live.
  • Real-Time Loss Telemetry: Track prevented courier penalties and blocked checkout attempts in admin dashboard.
v2.1.0 — Freight & PO Box Locker Hubs March 2026

Expanded carrier regex patterns for private locker hubs, parcel drop-boxes, and courier depots.

  • USPS & Global Postal Matchers: Detected over 30 variations of PO Box and postal locker formats.
  • Zero Database Roundtrips: Implemented shop metafields fast cache for instant rule resolution.
v2.0.0 — WebAssembly Engine Architecture January 2026

Complete platform migration to Shopify Functions (purchase.validation.run), eliminating legacy client-side script tags and theme bloat entirely.
